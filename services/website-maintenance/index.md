A live website still needs attention. I provide website maintenance and support for small businesses in Syracuse, Marcellus and Central New York, with ongoing plans or one-time fixes based on your site and platform.

## Help keeping your site current

- Update service descriptions, hours, photos and contact information.
- Review and apply software or dependency updates where applicable.
- Set up and review backups for the parts of your site that need them.
- Help with hosting, domain renewals and SSL certificate issues.
- Investigate broken pages, forms and integrations.
- Review performance issues and recommend practical improvements.

## A plan that fits your website

A static brochure site has different maintenance needs from an online store or a custom application. I review your platform and access, then agree on the work included, update frequency and support expectations. [See the monthly care plans](/#care) for a starting point.

## One-time fixes are welcome

You do not need a full rebuild to fix an expired certificate, move a domain or update an outdated page. Send me the issue and I will explain the options and quote the work before starting.

If your site needs a larger refresh, I also offer [website design](/services/website-design/), [SEO improvements](/services/seo/) and [custom web app development](/services/custom-web-apps/).

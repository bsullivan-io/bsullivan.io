When someone searches for your services, your website needs to explain what you do and where you work. I help small businesses in Syracuse, Marcellus and Central New York improve their search foundations and understand how customers find them.

## Start with the search basics

- Review page titles, descriptions, headings and internal links.
- Check whether important pages can be crawled and indexed.
- Identify technical issues such as broken links and conflicting canonical URLs.
- Organize service pages around real customer questions.
- Review local business details for accuracy and consistency.
- Set up or review Google Search Console and analytics reporting.

## Local search and business listings

For businesses serving a local area, I can help with Google Business Profile setup or cleanup where eligible, service-area information and links between your website and business profiles. Your website content should describe your actual services and the places you serve.

## Reporting you can use

We agree on what matters before making changes: relevant search impressions, visits, contact requests or bookings. I explain what the available data shows and recommend next steps. Search visibility takes time, and no position in Google is guaranteed.

## Content with a purpose

Useful service descriptions, answers to customer questions and real project examples give visitors more reasons to contact you.

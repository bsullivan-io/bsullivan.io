Sometimes your business needs more than a set of web pages. I build custom web applications and integrations for businesses in Syracuse, Marcellus and Central New York, drawing on 15+ years of full-stack engineering experience.

## Tools built around your workflow

- Appointment booking and service request workflows.
- Quote calculators that use your pricing rules to provide estimates.
- Online ordering and Stripe payment integrations.
- Customer portals and internal dashboards.
- Connections between existing systems through APIs.
- Automation that reduces repetitive data entry.

## Integrate first, build where it helps

An established booking or commerce platform may already cover your needs. I can connect it to your website and build the missing pieces when a standard tool cannot support your process. We discuss access, data handling and ongoing costs before choosing an approach.

## Start with one useful workflow

We define the people using the tool, the information it needs and the result it should produce. I scope an initial version, build and test the workflow, and review it with you before launch. Pricing depends on the features, integrations and support needs.

Your website should make it easy to understand what you offer and take the next step. I design and build websites for small businesses in Syracuse, Marcellus and Central New York, working directly with you from the first conversation through launch.

## A website built around your business

- Clear pages for your services, location and contact details.
- Responsive layouts that work on phones, tablets and computers.
- Contact forms, quote requests and online booking integrations where needed.
- Social media links so customers can find your Facebook and Instagram accounts.
- Search-friendly page titles, headings and content structure.
- Domain, hosting and SSL setup as part of the agreed project scope.

## From an idea to a live website

We start with your customers, the questions they ask and what you want them to do on the site. I then plan the pages, build the design and review it with you. Before launch, I check the layout, navigation and contact paths.

Already have a site? I can review it and recommend whether a focused update or a rebuild makes sense. Pricing is quoted up front based on the pages, content and features you need.

## Features that go beyond a brochure

A salon may need appointment booking. A contractor may need a detailed quote request. A shop may need an online store. I can connect an existing service or build a [custom web application](/services/custom-web-apps/) when your workflow needs more flexibility.

After launch, I can help with [SEO](/services/seo/) and [ongoing website maintenance](/services/website-maintenance/).

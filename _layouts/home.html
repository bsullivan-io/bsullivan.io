01 Common issues

Problems I can fix.

Websites that have run for years without regular maintenance tend to run into the same few problems. Here are the ones I see most often.

1

Your site is down or shows a security warning

This is usually an expired SSL certificate, lapsed hosting, or a domain that wasn't renewed. I'll get it working again and set up monitoring so it doesn't happen again.

2

Facebook is your only website

Facebook pages don't show up well in Google search. I can build a website that displays your Facebook posts, so you can keep posting in one place.

3

Your site is outdated and hard to update

I rebuild older sites so they work on phones and load quickly. After that, you can email or text me changes and I'll make them.

4

Your Google listing is wrong or missing

Old hours, a disconnected phone number, or no listing at all. I claim and update your Google Business Profile and other directory listings.
